Scrub the timeline or click a gold marker on the map.

    A map-first companion to the Dow / Bank timelines: scrub month by month across continental Europe and watch approximate control shift from Poland to Berlin.

    How to use

    • Drag the scrubber (or use ← →) to move through 18 curated map frames.
    • Click gold markers for that month’s war, diplomacy, or culture beat (roughly one+ per month, denser in crisis months).
    • Toggle Perspective for a light “1940s framing” note — not a classified big-board reconstruction.
    • Optional sparkline: approximate Allied merchant tonnage sunk per month (Battle of the Atlantic).

    Honest limits

    • Front lines and occupation are teaching approximations (country fills + a few schematic overlays), not day-accurate GIS.
    • Modern borders stand in for 1939 states (Yugoslavia, Czechoslovakia, USSR republics).
    • Pacific theatre is out of scope for v1.
    • Holocaust / mass-death topics appear only as sober notes — not as gamified map dots.

    Sources
